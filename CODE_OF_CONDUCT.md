# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Light Code to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background
or identity. We pledge to make taking part a harassment-free experience for all.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, especially with newcomers
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Owning mistakes and learning from them

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission
- Trolling, or any other conduct that is unwelcome in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you
represent the project).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that break this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the
[Digital Scape MY GitHub organisation](https://github.com/digitalscapemy), or open a private
security advisory if it involves a security matter. Every report will be reviewed and handled
promptly and confidentially.
